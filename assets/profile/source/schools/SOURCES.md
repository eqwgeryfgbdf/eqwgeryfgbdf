# Official university marks

These marks identify the universities in the profile owner's education history. They remain the respective universities' marks, with their original proportions and colors, displayed on a white backing in both themes.

- **National Taipei University of Technology (Taipei Tech)**: [official logo download page](https://sec.ntut.edu.tw/p/412-1027-8559.php?Lang=zh-tw), [vector PDF](https://sec.ntut.edu.tw/var/file/27/1027/img/920/TaipeiTechLogo2.pdf). The large color Taipei Tech mark was isolated from page 1 (PDF coordinates 86,441–517,699) and exported to `ntut.svg` as 12 vector paths.
- **Lunghwa University of Science and Technology**: [official university-mark page](https://secretary.lhu.edu.tw/p/412-1035-1562.php?Lang=zh-tw), [official source-file share](https://ncs.lhu.edu.tw/index.php/s/DHy2p8HrToJ4HHj?dir=/2.LOGO). Source: `LOGO_outline(原始檔).ai`, a PDF-compatible Illustrator file. The upper-left LHU symbol and 龍華 lettering were isolated from page 1 (PDF coordinates 99,67–273,191) and exported to `lhu.svg` as 34 vector paths.

Retrieved 2026-11-01. Conversion preserved original path geometry with PyMuPDF; neither mark is a traced bitmap or an AI-generated imitation. The SVGs contain no raster images or external dependencies.
